## Project Overview

Supri Spinach Stock Research is a **stock research and analysis platform** specifically designed for the Indonesian stock market (IDX). It provides comprehensive daily reports and in-depth research capabilities for stock analysis.

[View Repository on GitHub](https://github.com/milyas26/supri-spinach-stock-research)

- **Daily Stock Reports**: Automated generation of daily market reports with stock analysis and recommendations.
- **Deep Research**: Detailed analysis reports for individual stocks with technical and fundamental insights.
- **Markdown-Based Content**: All reports and research content are stored in Markdown format for easy editing and version control.
- **IDX Focus**: Specialized for Indonesian stock market analysis with local market data and insights.

## Core Features

The platform provides essential tools for stock research and analysis:

- **Report Generation**: Automated daily reports summarizing market movements and stock performance.
- **Stock Analysis**: Individual stock research with technical indicators, price targets, and recommendations.
- **Content Management**: Organized content structure with separate sections for reports and deep research.
- **Search & Filter**: Easy navigation through historical reports and research documents.

## Technical Architecture

Built with modern web technologies for optimal performance:

- **Next.js 16**: Server-side rendering and static generation for fast page loads.
- **React 19**: Latest React features for building interactive user interfaces.
- **Tailwind CSS 4**: Utility-first CSS framework for rapid UI development.
- **Markdown Processing**: Using Remark and Gray Matter for parsing and rendering Markdown content.
- **TypeScript**: Type-safe development with full TypeScript support.

## Content Structure

The platform organizes content in a logical hierarchy:

- **Daily Reports**: Date-based reports (e.g., Report_2026-06-03.md) containing market summaries.
- **Deep Research**: Individual stock analysis files (e.g., BRPT_2026-06-03.md, ADMR_2026-06-04.md).
- **Organized Storage**: Content stored in separate directories for easy management and retrieval.

## Use Cases

- **Individual Investors**: Track daily market movements and get stock recommendations.
- **Stock Analysts**: Generate comprehensive research reports for specific stocks.
- **Portfolio Managers**: Review historical analysis and track research progress.
- **Market Researchers**: Access organized market data and analysis for research purposes.

## Benefits

- **Centralized Research**: All stock research and reports in one accessible platform.
- **Historical Access**: Easy access to past reports and analysis for trend identification.
- **Markdown Flexibility**: Content can be edited with any text editor and version controlled.
- **Fast Performance**: Next.js ensures quick loading and smooth navigation.

Supri Spinach Stock Research provides a powerful platform for Indonesian stock market analysis. With its focus on daily reporting and in-depth research capabilities, it serves as a comprehensive tool for investors and analysts seeking to make informed decisions in the IDX market.
